FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need To Know.

Have questions about SocialVault? Find answers to commonly asked questions about Instagram Reel downloads, Story view privacy, Facebook tools, and data security below.

What is SocialVault? +
SocialVault is a premium all-in-one media intelligence suite built for creators, agencies, and digital marketers. It provides powerful downloaders, hashtag scrapers, and analytics tools for Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube without requiring any software installation.
Which social platforms are supported? +
SocialVault fully supports public media extraction across three major platforms: Instagram (Reels, Stories, Highlights, Hashtags), Facebook (Reels, Posts, Stories), and YouTube (Shorts, Video, Thumbnails).
What tools are available in the suite? +
We provide 10+ specialized tools including: Instagram Downloader (Reels, Stories, Highlights), Facebook Downloader (Reels, Stories, Posts), YouTube Downloader (Shorts, Videos, HD Thumbnails), and deep data extractors like our Hashtag & Comment Scrapers.
Do I need to log in to my social media accounts? +
Absolutely not. SocialVault respects your privacy and security. You never need to provide your Instagram, Facebook, or Google passwords. Simply paste public URLs directly into our tools to start downloading.
Are the videos downloaded with a watermark? +
No! All videos—whether they are Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, or YouTube Shorts—are downloaded in their original uncompressed MP4 format with absolutely zero watermarks.
What video quality can I expect? +
We prioritize ultra-high definition. Whenever available on the platform, SocialVault extracts media in original 1080p HD, 2K, or 4K resolution, ensuring crisp quality for professional content repurposing.
Are my downloads 100% anonymous? +
Yes! Watching or saving public Instagram Stories, Facebook Reels, or YouTube videos via SocialVault leaves zero digital footprint. The original creator will never know you viewed or downloaded their content.
Is SocialVault compatible with mobile devices? +
Yes! SocialVault is a 100% cloud-based web application. It works flawlessly across all devices, including iPhones, Android smartphones, iPads, Macs, and Windows PCs.
